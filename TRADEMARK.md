# Trademark & Brand Policy

Soundpost's **source code** is free and open under the [GNU GPLv3](LICENSE) — you can use, study,
modify, and redistribute it under those terms. The project's **name and logo are different.** They
are the identity of the project and the community, and this policy keeps them meaningful.

This is a friendly, common-sense policy in the spirit of many open-source projects. It is not legal
advice, and "Soundpost" is not (yet) a registered trademark — but the project asserts common-law
rights in the name and logo as used here.

## The marks

- **The name:** **Soundpost** (and the stylized wordmark `SOUND`**`POST`**).
- **The logo:** the interlocking "S" soundwave mark in [`assets/`](assets/) (`logo.svg` /
  `logo.png`), in its orange→violet gradient form.

## ✅ You may, without asking

- Use the name **to refer to the project** — "built with Soundpost," "a Soundpost plugin,"
  "works with Soundpost," blog posts, tutorials, reviews.
- Use the logo to **link to or credit** this project.
- Redistribute **unmodified** builds of Soundpost under their real name.
- Build and publish **plugins, themes, and visualizers** and describe them as "for Soundpost."
- Fork the code (it's GPLv3!) for your own use and development.

## 🚫 Please do not, without permission

- Use the name or logo in a way that implies **official endorsement, affiliation, or authorship** by
  the Soundpost project when there isn't any.
- Use the name or logo for a **competing product, distribution, or paid service** — if you ship a
  modified version publicly, **give it a different name and logo** so users aren't confused about
  what they're getting or who supports it.
- Alter the logo's proportions or recolor it in ways that misrepresent the brand, or combine it with
  other marks to create a confusingly similar one.
- Register the name or logo (or anything confusingly similar) as your own trademark, domain, app-store
  listing, or social handle.

## Naming your fork or plugin

- **Plugins / themes / visualizers:** call them what you like and add "for Soundpost." Good:
  *"Ripple — a visualizer for Soundpost."* Avoid names that read as official, like *"Soundpost Pro."*
- **Forks you distribute:** pick your own name and logo. You keep all your GPLv3 rights to the code.

## Assets

Brand assets live in [`assets/`](assets/). Use `logo.svg` for anything scalable. Don't stretch,
recolor, or add effects beyond what's provided.

## Questions

Not sure whether a use is okay? Open a [discussion](../../discussions) and ask — the answer is usually
"yes, go ahead." We want people using and talking about Soundpost; this policy only exists so the name
keeps meaning one thing.
