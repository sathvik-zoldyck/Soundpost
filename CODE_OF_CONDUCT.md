# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the
Fader community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being kind, patient, and welcoming — especially to newcomers.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project.
- Showing empathy toward other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Trolling, deliberate intimidation, or sustained disruption.
- Any conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the project maintainers via a private issue or the contact listed in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated
promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and
security of the reporter.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and
other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.
